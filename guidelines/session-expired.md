# Session expired

## Sign in on the same page

<!-- rule: sign-in-same-page -->

**Rule:** When a session ends, open a sign-in dialog over the current page.

**Why:** The reader can see where work will continue.

**Do:** Open “Sign in again” over Orders.

**Don't:** Send the reader to a sign-in page.

## Keep the work

<!-- rule: keep-work -->

**Rule:** After sign-in, return the reader to the same place with the same input and filters.

**Why:** The reader loses time when they must set the search and filter again.

**Do:** Keep the search text and the pending filter.

**Don't:** Clear the search text or the filter.

## Say that the session ended

<!-- rule: say-session-ended -->

**Rule:** Say that the session ended and that sign-in continues the work.

**Why:** The reader needs to know why the dialog opened.

**Do:** Say “Your session ended. Sign in to keep working.”

**Don't:** Say only “Sign in required.”

## Keep the shell and title

<!-- rule: skeleton-behind-dialog -->

**Rule:** Keep the page shell and title, and replace page data with skeleton shapes until sign-in.

**Why:** The reader keeps their place without seeing protected data.

**Do:** Keep Orders and show a table-shaped skeleton.

**Don't:** Blank the whole page behind the dialog.

## Keep a way back in

<!-- rule: lasting-sign-in -->

**Rule:** If the reader closes the dialog, keep a lasting notice with a Sign in button on the page.

**Why:** The reader can sign in when ready without losing the page.

**Do:** Show “You are signed out” with Sign in.

**Don't:** Remove the dialog and show no sign-in action.
