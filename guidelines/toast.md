# Toast

## Lift the stack above a bottom bar

<!-- rule: above-bottom-bar -->

**Rule:** Give the toast stack the inset of a bar at the bottom of the page.

**Why:** A notice on the bar hides its actions, and a page style on the stack breaks in a later kit release.

**Do:** Show “2 files archived” above the selection bar.

**Don't:** Show the notice over the selection bar.

## Let the ring show the time left

<!-- rule: ring-time-left -->

**Rule:** Let the ring on a timed Undo show the time left, with no seconds in the text.

**Why:** A count in the text is out of date while the reader reads it.

**Do:** Show “Seed list archived” with the ring on Undo.

**Don't:** Write “Undo in 8 seconds” in the notice.
