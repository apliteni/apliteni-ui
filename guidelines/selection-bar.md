# Selection bar

## Paint the bar in the other theme

<!-- rule: other-theme -->

**Rule:** Show a selection bar dark on a light page and light on a dark page.

**Why:** The bar floats over the rows, and in their colours it reads as one more row.

**Do:** Show the bar dark under the light documents table.

**Don't:** Show the bar in the white of the table under it.

## Apply each pick at once

<!-- rule: apply-at-once -->

**Rule:** Apply a pick to the selected rows at once, keep them selected, and say what changed in a notice.

**Why:** An Apply step asks for a second act, and a silent change leaves the reader to check each row.

**Do:** Pick Ready for Status, and show “Status: Ready” with “Updated 2 documents.”

**Don't:** Add an Apply button after the Status list.

## List the rest in More by name

<!-- rule: more-by-name -->

**Rule:** Keep the actions that fit on the bar, and list the rest in More by their names.

**Why:** A bare value such as Avery does not say which action it sets.

**Do:** List Archive and Owner in More, and open the owners from Owner.

**Don't:** List Avery and Blair in More under no action name.
