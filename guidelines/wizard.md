# Wizard

## Show each step's state

<!-- rule: step-states -->

**Rule:** Give each step one of four states, done, current, blocked or to do, with a mark and a word.

**Why:** A shape and a word keep the state clear without colour.

**Do:** Show a check in a grey circle and the word Done, with no text that says the step is complete.

**Don't:** Show the state with colour alone.

## Open one step

<!-- rule: one-open -->

**Rule:** Keep one step open, and open the step to work on first.

**Why:** The reader sees one step's words and action at a time.

**Do:** Open the next step when the reader finishes a step.

**Don't:** Open every step at once.

## Say why the final action waits

<!-- rule: final-action -->

**Rule:** Keep the final action off until each earlier step is done, and say why above it.

**Why:** An action that is off does not tell the reader what to finish.

**Do:** Write "Complete all four checks to close this month." above Close month.

**Don't:** Hide Close month until the checks are done.

## Name what blocks a step

<!-- rule: blocked-reason -->

**Rule:** Say what blocks a step, and give the action that removes the block.

**Why:** The word Blocked alone does not tell the reader what to do.

**Do:** Write "The receipt from Cedar Tools is missing." with an Add button.

**Don't:** Show Blocked with no reason.
