# Three-column page

## Use three columns for steps over a list

<!-- rule: three-columns -->

**Rule:** Put a list, its ordered steps and the work of the open step on one three-column page.

**Why:** The reader sees the record, the next step and its rows together.

**Do:** Show the months, the month's checks and the rows of the open check.

**Don't:** Open the checks in a drawer over the rows.

## Keep the work half wide

<!-- rule: column-widths -->

**Rule:** Give the list and the steps one half of at most 560px, and give the work half the rest.

**Why:** The rows need the room, and the steps read best as a short column.

**Do:** Give the list one third and the steps two thirds of their half.

**Don't:** Split the page into three equal columns.

## Collapse in order

<!-- rule: collapse-order -->

**Rule:** At 820px, show the list as its own view and the steps above the work; at 390px, show one area at a time and open on the steps.

**Why:** The steps are the reader's place in the task, so they stay first.

**Do:** Put Months, Checks and Rows links under the title.

**Don't:** Squeeze three columns onto a phone.
