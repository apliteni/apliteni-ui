# Three-column page

## Chain the columns from parent to child

<!-- rule: three-columns -->

**Rule:** Make column 1 the parents, column 2 the children of the selected parent, and column 3 the selected child.

**Why:** The reader sees where the open item comes from.

**Do:** Show the folders, then the files of the selected folder, then the open file.

**Don't:** Put three unrelated columns on one page.

## Mark the selected parent

<!-- rule: selected-parent -->

**Rule:** Keep the selected paint on the selected item in columns 1 and 2, and end each parent row with a chevron.

**Why:** The reader sees which parent each column belongs to.

**Do:** Mark the selected folder and the open file.

**Don't:** Remove a parent's mark when the reader works in the next column.

## Replace the children when the parent changes

<!-- rule: replace-children -->

**Rule:** When the reader selects a new parent, replace each column after it.

**Why:** Children of the old parent beside the new parent give the reader wrong data.

**Do:** Replace the files and the open file for a new folder.

**Don't:** Keep the old folder's files beside a new folder.

**Except:** A parent with no children gives column 3's space to column 2, which says what is missing.

## Keep the child column wide

<!-- rule: column-widths -->

**Rule:** Give column 1 240px, column 2 from 260px to 320px, and column 3 the rest.

**Why:** The open item needs the room, and the lists read best as short columns.

**Do:** Let each column scroll alone, with a full-height line between columns.

**Don't:** Split the page into three equal columns.

## Show two columns, then one

<!-- rule: collapse-order -->

**Rule:** At 820px show the open column and its parent, and at 390px the open column only, under a back link that names the hidden parent.

**Why:** The reader still goes up the chain one step at a time.

**Do:** Put a back link to the folders above the files on a phone.

**Don't:** Squeeze three columns onto a phone.

## Give each column one header row

<!-- rule: column-header -->

**Rule:** Start each column with a header row of one height: the title, then an optional count and tools.

**Why:** The titles stand on one line, so the chain reads across the page.

**Do:** Put a column's sorting, view and quick filters in a menu on its title.

**Don't:** Put a bar of view options between the title and the list.
