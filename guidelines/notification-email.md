# Notification email

## Link the task and the settings

<!-- rule: task-mail -->

**Rule:** Name the task event, link to each task, provide plain text, and end with a notification settings link.

**Why:** The reader acts from the link and stops unwanted mail from the last line.

**Do:** Send New task: Prepare the audit folder, with a task link and Email settings last.

**Don't:** Send You have updates, with no link.
