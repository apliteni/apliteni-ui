# Spacing

## Use two gaps

<!-- rule: two-gaps -->

**Rule:** Use 8px between parts of one group and 32px between groups.

**Why:** Two distances show what belongs together, and a reader never has to compare a third.

**Do:** Set the filter row, the view strip and their table 8px apart, and the next section 32px below.

**Don't:** Add a third gap, such as 20px between sections.

## Open the phone's inner gap

<!-- rule: two-gaps-phone -->

**Rule:** On a touch phone, every gap inside a group of small controls is 20px.

**Why:** The tap floor already opens chip lines to 20px.

**Do:** At 390, 20px from the filters to the strip and the table.

**Don't:** Keep 8px above a table under 20px chip lines.

**Except:** The view strip wraps 4px apart; its tabs meet the floor.

## Keep a title on its block

<!-- rule: title -->

**Rule:** Keep a page or card title 8px above what it names, at every width.

**Why:** A title is part of its group, so it takes the inner gap.

**Do:** Keep a card's first line 8px under its title, inside the 32px around the card.

**Don't:** Give a title the 32px between groups.

## Put text on the column edge

<!-- rule: text-edge -->

**Rule:** A control that draws no box starts its first label on the page title's edge.

**Why:** With nothing drawn around it, a 16px indent reads as a misaligned line.

**Do:** Let the view strip's highlight and ring hang into the margin.

**Don't:** Move a filter chip or a table off the column edge.
