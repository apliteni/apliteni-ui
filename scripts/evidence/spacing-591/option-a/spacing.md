# Spacing

## Widen each level

<!-- rule: ladder -->

**Rule:** Make each gap one scale step wider than the gaps inside what it separates.

**Why:** Parts that sit closer than their neighbours read as one block.

**Do:** At 1280, 8px between chip lines, 12px above the table, 16px between sections, 20px under the head.

**Don't:** Set a filter row as far from its table as from the next section.

## Climb from the phone's row gap

<!-- rule: ladder-phone -->

**Rule:** On a touch phone, start the ladder at the 20px the tap floor puts between chip lines.

**Why:** Desktop steps would sit closer than the lines inside them.

**Do:** At 390, 24px above the table, 32px between sections and 40px under the page head.

**Don't:** Keep 12px above a table under chip lines 20px apart.

## Keep a title on its block

<!-- rule: title -->

**Rule:** Keep a page or card title 8px above what it names, at every width.

**Why:** A title belongs to its block, so it takes the smallest step.

**Do:** Keep the introduction 8px under the title while the head sits 40px above the table on a phone.

**Don't:** Give a title the gap between sections.

## Start controls on the column edge

<!-- rule: column-edge -->

**Rule:** Start every control row's box on the page column's edge.

**Why:** One left edge keeps the page on one grid.

**Do:** Leave the view strip's first label 16px in, over the table's first column.

**Don't:** Indent a filter row from the page title.

**Except:** The view strip wraps 4px apart; its tabs already reach the tap floor.
