#275 — the four page limits

Each board is the same finance screen, drawn by the kit's own appShell(), with one limit changed. Everything on the guidelines page is settled; these four are numbers somebody has to choose. The screens are drawn at 36% — the proportions are true, the pixels are not. This comparison board is not itself a page under the rules it argues about: it holds nine screens, so it holds nine page titles and nine of every landmark the shell draws.

1. How many cards may a page stack?

Recommended: six — at the limit a page still reads as one screen

2. May a page carry two primary actions?

Recommended: no — one filled button, the rest ranked under it

3. How deep may the heading outline go?

Recommended: h3 — page, card, group inside a card

4. What density may a data page take?

Recommended: dense for every table on the page, or none of them
